# Privacy policy

This policy covers the Ukrainian Canada News app (the "app"), which publishes posts to the Ukrainian Canada
Telegram channel (t.me/UkrainianCanada) and the Ukrainian Canada Facebook Page (facebook.com/canadaua).

**What the app does.** It publishes posts that the channel's editors have written and approved to the
channel and the Page, and adds a comment to each Page post with a link to the Telegram channel.

**What data it collects.** None about readers or visitors. The app does not collect, store, sell or share
personal information. It does not read messages, profiles or comments of Facebook or Telegram users.

**What it stores.** Only what it needs to run: the access token for the Page, kept as an encrypted secret,
and a list of the posts it has prepared and published.

**Third parties.** Posts are published through the Facebook Graph API and the Telegram Bot API. Their use is
governed by Meta's and Telegram's own privacy policies.

**Deleting data.** There is no personal data to delete. To ask anything about this policy, write to the
channel at https://t.me/UkrainianCanada or through the Facebook Page.

Last updated: November 22, 2026.
